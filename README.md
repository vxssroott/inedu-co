## Overview

**INEDU & CO Professional Services** is the official corporate website for a duly registered Nigerian professional services and consultancy firm.

The platform is designed to establish trust, strengthen corporate presence, and provide clients with direct access to the company’s professional services, executive contact channels, and business credibility.

Built with a premium executive design approach, the website reflects professionalism, authority, confidentiality, and corporate excellence.

---

## Company Profile

INEDU & CO Professional Services provides reliable, ethical, and results-driven support to individuals, SMEs, and corporate organizations.

The firm delivers tailored solutions across consultancy, recovery, verification, facilitation, and commission agency services with a strong focus on professionalism, confidentiality, and compliance.

---

## Core Services

### Professional Services
Training on Address Verification (AVR), Internal Control, Collections and Recovery, and related professional support.

### Management & Business Consultancy
Audited Financial Statements, Financial Advisory Services, and strategic business advisory support.

### Debt Recovery & Portfolio Resolution
Professional recovery support, portfolio resolution, and structured debt recovery services.

### Commission Agency Services
Sale and purchase of MFB operating licenses, Trade Finance Instruments Deals, PSSP Acquisition, and general commission-based transactions.

### Business & Address Verifications
Reliable verification services for individuals, businesses, addresses, and related documentation.

### Loan Facilitation and Credit Support
Support for loan facilitation, credit access, and financial opportunity processing.
